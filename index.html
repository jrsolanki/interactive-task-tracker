    How it works

    • Add a task with the input above — press Enter or click Add Task.
    • Complete a task by ticking its checkbox; tick again to reopen it.
    • Delete any task with its ✕ button, or remove all finished ones at once with Clear completed.
    • Filter between All, Active and Completed to focus on what matters.
    • Persistence: tasks are stored in the browser's localStorage, so reloading the page keeps your list.
